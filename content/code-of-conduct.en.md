KCD Argentina adopts the [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md). By participating in the event, you agree to follow this code.

## Expected behavior

- Participate in an open and welcoming way.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism with empathy.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, offensive or discriminatory comments.
- Intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome physical contact.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, contact the organizing team at **kcd.argentina@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
